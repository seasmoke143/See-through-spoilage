User Manual: How to Scan an Onion

  1. Open the App & Position the Mat: Open the Smart Onion Grading app and place your phone on a stable surface facing the reference mat.
  2. Place the Onion: Look at your phone screen. You will see a guide box. Place the onion directly inside this box on the mat.
  3. Wait for the Prompt: Wait for the app's voice or text prompt confirming the onion is in the right spot and not moving.
  4. Capture the Images: Press the "Capture" button on the screen. The app will instantly take three pictures: a normal photo (RGB), an infrared photo (NIR), and a depth photo.
  5. Wait for Processing: Keep the onion still for a brief moment while the app checks the photos.
  6. Retake if Prompted: If the app says "Invalid Capture" (e.g., the photo is blurry, too dark, or the onion is off-center), simply adjust the onion and press Capture again.
  7. Automatic Cleanup: Once a good picture is taken, the app will automatically fix the lighting and remove the background. You do not need to do anything here.
  8. Completion: You will see a "Success" or "Processing Complete" message on the screen. You can now remove the onion and place the next one to repeat the process.
